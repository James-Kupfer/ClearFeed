# Haiku 4.5 vs Haiku 5.5 on ClearFeed ingest (A/B, 2026-10-08)

**Setup.** 50 stored Gmail records (stratified across 9 subject labels from a pool of 2,226 in the last 90 days), run through
ClearFeed's own `summarize.md` / `classify.md` prompts and parsing on `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` and `claude-haiku-5-5`
(production defaults: no effort set, so 5.5 runs adaptive thinking at medium). Per record and model: summarize; classify its own
summary; classify the stored production summary (isolates classifier differences). Read-only; nothing written to the DB.
Escalation to Sonnet was recorded as a *would-escalate* flag, not executed. Summaries were judged blind by Sonnet 5.5 (order randomized).
n=50, one run per model, no human ground truth. Harness: scratchpad `ab_haiku.py` / `ab_analyze.py` (not in repo).

Classifier agreement (same stored summary): 4.5 vs 5.5 subject label 68%, purpose label 94%, `actionable` flag 100%,
tag Jaccard 0.29. Against the stored production label: on the 35 records Haiku 4.5 alone produced, 4.5 re-run agrees 31/35 and
5.5 27/35 (4.5's figure includes its own run-to-run consistency). On the 15 records Sonnet escalation produced, both agree 10/15.
Where the final stored label is Miscellaneous (6 records), 5.5 assigned a topical label in all 6.

## Reading
- Summaries: 5.5 is clearly better on this judge and much cheaper and faster.
- Classification: 5.5 is more decisive. It essentially never uses Miscellaneous and reports lower confidence, so the
  Misc and confidence<=2 escalation triggers fire far less (10% vs 26%). Fewer records get a Sonnet second look.
  Whether 5.5's topical labels on formerly-Misc items are right is not established by this test.
- No refusals seen in 50 records (6 Health, 6 Science, 5 Technology included); not a guarantee at volume.

## Open decision
Keep `haiku` floating (5.5) for summarize. For classify, either accept the new behavior, or hand-check the subject-label
disagreements (ids 3817, 2389, 1831, 4322, 2702, 2341, 4665, 2397, 3574, 2931, 3760, 4206, 3231, 3390, 1540, 3351), or pin
classify to `haiku-4.5` while keeping summarize on 5.5.

## Blind human review of the 16 subject-label disagreements (same day)
Reviewer saw subject, sender, stored executive summary and two unlabeled label sets (order randomized).
Picks: Haiku 4.5 = 6, Haiku 5.5 = 5, both acceptable = 2, neither = 3 (items 1 and 12 gave no alternative; for item 13, the Andrew Yang AI-dividends alert, the reviewer chose "Spam").

Pattern:
- **4.5 won on content-free notifications** (unsubscribe confirmation, two LinkedIn game invites, a survey request, an alert with
  no article text): 4.5 said Miscellaneous, 5.5 forced a topical label (Personal/Culture/Business).
- **5.5 won on content-bearing items 4.5 dumped in Miscellaneous** (paid research-study recruitment, a multi-item news alert) and
  on two topical calls (Sweaty Startup -> Business, Turchin/Japan -> Politics).
- Production's current flow (4.5 labels, Miscellaneous escalates to Sonnet) matched the reviewer on 6 of the 10 records Sonnet
  had finalized. 5.5 alone removes that safety net: it never says Miscellaneous and flags only 3 of the 15 records production
  escalated.

n=16 (selected for disagreement, one reviewer), so it shows the failure mode, not a rate.

## Test of a prompt nudge for Miscellaneous (same day, classifier only, identical stored summaries, 3 arms x 100 emails)
Nudge (appended to step 4 of DECISION ORDER in classify.md): "An item whose summary has no topical substance is also Miscellaneous:
a bare notification or confirmation (e.g. an unsubscribe confirmation), an invite to a platform feature or game, a feedback-survey
request, or an alert or digest whose summary says the linked article or page text was unavailable (only navigation, login pages or a
bare headline). Never borrow a subject from the sender, the platform, or keywords in a headline; if the summary leaves nothing to
classify, label Miscellaneous." Sets: the original 50 (includes the 16 reviewed blind; nudge was written after seeing them, so
in-sample) and 50 fresh emails (out-of-sample, scored against Sonnet-confirmed stored labels as a proxy).

| | Haiku 4.5 | Haiku 5.5 | Haiku 5.5 + nudge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matches reviewer's picks (13 clear picks) | 10 | 7 | 9 |
| Miscellaneous used, original / fresh (of 50) | 8 / 6 | 1 / 3 | 9 / 6 |
| Would escalate to Sonnet, original / fresh | 11 / 6 | 9 / 6 | 13 / 10 |
| Catches stored-Misc records (12 total) | 9 | 3 | 10 |
| Misc precision vs stored (proxy) | 64% | 75% | 67% |

The nudge changed 16 of 100 labels. 10 moved to Miscellaneous (6 matched Sonnet's stored Misc; 4 were Google Alerts with real
content that Sonnet had labeled topically - production would send these to Sonnet anyway, so that error is cheap). The other 5-6
changes are non-Misc flips at about the 5.5 run-to-run noise level (46 of 50 unchanged on rerun; Haiku 4.5: 40 of 50).
